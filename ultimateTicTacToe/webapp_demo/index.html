Ultimate Tic Tac Toe - Rules

  • The game is played on a 3x3 grid of 3x3 Tic Tac Toe boards — 81 cells in total.
  • Players take turns as X and O. The first move can be anywhere.
  • The position you play in a local 3x3 board determines the local board your opponent must play in next.
  • If that target board is already won or full, the opponent can play anywhere.
  • Winning a local board happens as in regular Tic Tac Toe (3 in a row).
  • You win the game by winning 3 local boards in a row on the global grid.
  • If all local boards are filled without a global winner, the game is a draw.
Tip: Think locally *and* globally. Blocking your opponent in one board could send them to a deadly spot on another!
